Privacy Policy

1. General Information

Protecting your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how data is processed when you use the FootballManager.IO app (web, iOS, and Android).

The app can be used without providing personal data beyond what is technically necessary to operate the service.

2. Account Registration

To use the game, users register with:

  • a username
  • a password
  • an email address

Passwords are stored only in encrypted and hashed form and cannot be read by the operator.

The email address is used for the following purposes:

  • account-recovery features (for example "forgot password")
  • email confirmation when an address is added or changed
  • important account-related notifications
  • occasional product updates such as release notes about new features

Email addresses are not shared with third parties and are not used for advertising or analytics. When a new email address is added or changed, a confirmation link is sent to that address and the new address only becomes active after the user clicks the link.

Users can disable non-essential emails (such as release notes) at any time in the account settings. Essential emails — password recovery and address confirmation — are always sent regardless of this setting, because they are required to operate the account safely. Users can also update or remove their email address at any time via the account settings. When an account is deleted, the associated email address is permanently removed.

3. User Content, Messaging & Uploads

FootballManager.IO includes social features that let users create and share content. The following user-generated content is stored on our servers:

  • Forum – posts and comments, including any images you attach
  • Friends feed – posts, comments, likes, and attached images
  • Private chat – direct one-to-one messages between users, including attached images
  • Profile – an optional avatar image and a free-text team/club description

Content posted in the forum and friends feed is visible to other users of the game. Private chat messages are only visible to you and the recipient. Please note that chat messages are stored on the server in unencrypted form (they are not end-to-end encrypted) so that they can be delivered and shown across your devices; you should therefore not share sensitive personal information through the chat.

Any text or image you submit through these features is provided voluntarily. You are responsible for the content you upload and must not upload images or text that infringe the rights of others. Uploaded images are stored as files on our server and are served back to the users who are allowed to see the corresponding content.

4. Push Notifications

If you use the iOS or Android app and allow notifications, the app registers a device-specific push token so that we can send you notifications (for example about match results, chat messages, or important account events). To deliver these notifications, the token and the notification content are transmitted through the platform push services:

  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) for iOS devices
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android devices

These providers act as processors for the delivery of push messages and process the data in accordance with their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). You can revoke the notification permission at any time in your device settings. Push tokens are deleted when you delete your account.

5. Local Storage & Analytics

This app does not use advertising cookies and does not embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics). It also does not track your behaviour across other websites or apps.

However, the app does use the following first-party mechanisms:

  • Local storage – the app stores technical information in your browser's/device's local storage (for example your login token, language preference, and a randomly generated, anonymous client identifier). This identifier is not linked to any third party and is used only to understand how visitors move through the app.
  • Usage analytics – we record which pages/screens are opened (together with the anonymous client identifier and, if you are logged in, your user id) in order to analyse the registration funnel and improve the product. We also record that a registration or login was attempted and whether it succeeded or was rejected, along with the technical reason for the rejection (for example "username already taken"). We never store the values you typed — neither the password nor the email address is recorded with these events. This data is processed only on our own server, is never sold or shared for advertising, and is deleted together with your account.
  • Error logging – to detect and fix bugs, the app may send technical diagnostic data (error messages, the affected URL, browser/OS type, and platform) to our server.

6. Mobile Apps (iOS & Android)

The mobile versions of FootballManager.IO are distributed via the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store. The apps require internet and network-state access to connect to the game server.

If you choose to upload an image (avatar, forum, friends feed, or chat), the app will request access to your photo library and/or camera for that purpose. Images are only uploaded when you actively select or take a picture. The apps do not access your contacts, microphone, or precise location, and they do not use any third-party advertising SDKs.

When you download the apps from the App Store or Google Play Store, Apple or Google may collect data in accordance with their respective privacy policies (Apple, Google). This data collection is outside our control.

7. Server Log Files & IP Addresses

For technical and security reasons, the server may automatically process limited data:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Browser and operating system (basic technical information)

In addition, we store the most recently used IP address and the derived country per platform (web, iOS, Android) on your account. The country is resolved locally on our server using an offline lookup database; no request is sent to an external geolocation service. This data is used solely to ensure the secure and stable operation of the service (for example fraud/abuse prevention) and to understand from which regions the game is used. It is not combined with data from third-party sources.

8. Data Sharing & Processors

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes. Personal data is only transmitted to third parties where this is technically necessary to provide the service, in particular:

  • Apple (APNs) and Google (FCM) for the delivery of push notifications (see section 4)
  • Our email provider for sending account-related and (optional) product emails (see section 2)

9. Data Storage & Deletion

User data (username and game-related data) is stored only for as long as the user account exists. Users may request deletion of their account and all associated data at any time.

Accounts that have been inactive for more than 21 days (no login) are automatically deleted. When an account is deleted, the associated team remains in the game as a bot-controlled team, but your personal data is permanently removed. This includes your username, password, email address, push tokens, and the content you created — forum posts and comments, friends-feed posts, comments and likes, private chat messages, news comments and likes, friend connections, and any images you uploaded (including your avatar). Your team is detached from your account.

10. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • request information about stored data
  • request correction or deletion of their data
  • object to data processing where legally applicable
